About Yucore Industrial

Guangzhou Xiaoyu Import and Export Co., Ltd., sourcing and exporting industrial components since 2014.

Yucore Industrial is the trading name of Guangzhou Xiaoyu Import and Export Co., Ltd., a sourcing and export company in Nansha, Guangzhou. Since 2014 we have helped factories, distributors, system integrators, panel builders and maintenance teams source electrical and automation parts from China, including PLCs, VFDs, sensors, servo motors, circuit breakers and control cabinet components. Hydraulic parts and MRO spares can be included in the same order. Buyers can request one replacement item or a multi-brand bill of materials. We quote within 24 hours when the manufacturer, exact model or part number, quantity, destination country and required delivery date are provided. Availability, condition, source, warranty, unit price, lead time, packing and freight are confirmed in the written quotation before an order is placed.
